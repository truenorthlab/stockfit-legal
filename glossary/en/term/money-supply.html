Money supply

How much money is circulating in the economy

In detail

Cash and deposits together, the pool of funds the economy can actually spend. When it grows, more money chases goods and services and prices tend to rise. It is one way to check whether the central bank's policy is really working.

Example

Growth of 3% on the year points to money expanding gently.
