Decentralised exchange

Swapping assets through code instead of a company

In detail

Orders are matched by a program on the blockchain rather than a firm, and the assets stay in your own wallet throughout. There is no account to open and no identity check, but flaws in the code and mistakes you make are entirely yours to bear.

Example

The program deducts a 0.3% fee automatically on every swap.
