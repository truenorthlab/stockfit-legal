Duration

How much a bond's price moves when interest rates move

In detail

Duration estimates how many percent a bond's price moves when interest rates move by one percentage point. The longer the time to maturity, the larger it gets and the more the price swings. That is why a long bond moves more than a short one on the same rate change. The earlier the interest arrives, the smaller the number.

Example

A bond with a duration of 5 falls about 5% in price when rates rise by 1%.
