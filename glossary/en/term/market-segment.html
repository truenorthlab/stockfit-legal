Market segment

How the Tokyo exchange splits listings into three markets

In detail

The Tokyo Stock Exchange is divided into Prime, Standard and Growth. Prime sets the highest bars for size and liquidity; Growth is mainly for companies still building their business. Each segment has its own thresholds for shareholders and free float, and failing them leads to a move down or a delisting. The segment tells you what stage the market expects of the company.

Example

Prime listings must keep a tradable market value of at least ¥10 billion, among other tests.
