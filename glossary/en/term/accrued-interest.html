Accrued interest

Interest built up since the last coupon date

In detail

Buy a bond mid-period and you pay the seller for the interest earned up to that day. You collect the full coupon next payment date, so it evens out. It sits on top of the quoted price, which makes the total you hand over larger than it looks.

Example

Buying a 2% bond half a year in means paying 1% extra on top.
