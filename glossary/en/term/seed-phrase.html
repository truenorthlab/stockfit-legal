Seed phrase

The ordered word list that restores a wallet

In detail

Twelve or twenty-four English words in a fixed order are enough to rebuild a wallet on any device. That also means anyone who reads them can move the assets, which is why storing them in photos or the cloud is discouraged. Lose the phrase and there is no other route back in.

Example

Getting one word of twenty-four out of order restores a different wallet, showing a balance of 0.
