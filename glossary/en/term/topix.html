TOPIX

A broad Tokyo index weighted by company size

In detail

TOPIX is calculated by the Tokyo Stock Exchange from the combined market value of its constituents. Where the Nikkei 225 leans on high-priced stocks, TOPIX leans on large ones. It covers far more names, so it is generally treated as the better picture of the market as a whole.

Example

A company worth ¥10 trillion moves TOPIX roughly 100 times more than one worth ¥100 billion.
