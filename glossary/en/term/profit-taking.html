Taking profit

Selling a gain on paper to make it real

In detail

Taking profit means selling a stock that has risen so the gain becomes real. Until you sell it is an unrealised gain, and it disappears if the price falls back. Selling also fixes the gain for tax purposes, which changes whether it can be offset against other losses in the same year. Like a stop-loss, many people decide the reason to sell in advance.

Example

Selling at $1,200 a share bought at 1,000 locks in $200 per share.
