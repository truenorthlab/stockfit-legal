Annual securities report

The fullest report a company files once a year

In detail

Listed companies in Japan file this report once a year, within three months of the fiscal year end. It arrives later than the earnings summary but carries far more: business risks, headcount, executive pay and the largest shareholders. It is the primary source when numbers alone are not enough, and anyone can read it free on EDINET.

Example

A company closing its books in March files within 3 months, by the end of June.
