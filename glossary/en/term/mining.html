Mining

Verifying transactions by computation and earning a reward

In detail

Mining is the work of checking that transactions are valid and writing them into the blockchain. The participant who first meets the condition receives newly issued coins as a reward. It takes a great deal of electricity and hardware, and the competition tightens as more participants join. In some designs the reward shrinks on a fixed schedule.

Example

In Bitcoin a block is settled roughly every 10 minutes, and that round's reward is issued.
