Moving average

The average price over a period, drawn as a line

In detail

A moving average takes the average closing price over the last N days and plots it, recalculating each day. It smooths daily noise so the larger direction is easier to see. Shorter periods react quickly to price; longer ones move slowly. Twenty-five, seventy-five, and two hundred days are common choices, but no period is the correct one.

Example

Closes of $1,000, $1,020, $980, $1,040, and $1,060 give a five-day average of $5,100 ÷ 5 = $1,020.
