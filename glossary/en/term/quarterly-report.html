Quarterly results

A scorecard published every three months

In detail

The year is split into four and sales and profit are reported each quarter. It shows change sooner than an annual report, but for seasonal businesses the figures swing about. The full-year outlook is often revised at the same time.

Example

$200 million in the first quarter against a $1 billion forecast is 20% of the way.
