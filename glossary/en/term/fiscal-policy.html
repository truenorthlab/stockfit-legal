Fiscal policy

A government steering the economy with spending and taxes

In detail

Money is pushed out through public works and payments, or demand is cooled by raising taxes. It is the counterpart to the central bank moving interest rates. Since it is funded by taxes or borrowing, keeping it up leaves a bill for later.

Example

A $100 billion package in a $5 trillion economy is about 2% of it.

Related terms

Easing and tightening GDP (Gross Domestic Product) Government bond
