Regular investing

Buying a fixed amount on a fixed schedule

In detail

Regular investing buys the same amount at the same interval, monthly for instance. You buy fewer units when prices are high and more when they are low, so your cost settles near the average. It removes the need to decide when to buy, though losses still accumulate in a market that keeps falling. Most people set it to run automatically.

Example

Buying $30,000 a month invests 360,000 across 12 purchases in a year.
