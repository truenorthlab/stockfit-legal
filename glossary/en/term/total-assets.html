Total assets

Everything the company owns, added up

In detail

Cash, inventory, factories and goodwill, all converted to money and summed. It always equals equity plus what the company owes. How much profit is squeezed out of that total is what return on assets measures.

Example

Equity of $40 million plus debts of $60 million makes $100 million of assets.
