Mid rate (TTM)

The bank's reference exchange rate for the day

In detail

Banks set a mid rate each business morning as the reference for that day's currency deals. Exchanging money at a bank means the mid rate plus a margin. The buy and sell rates sit symmetrically either side of the mid, and the gap between them is the fee you actually pay.

Example

A mid of 150 yen with a 1 yen margin gives 151 to buy and 149 to sell.

Related terms

Exchange rate Spread (FX) Foreign currency deposit
