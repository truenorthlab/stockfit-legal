Foreign exchange trading hours

A market that runs around the clock across regions

In detail

Asia, Europe and North America open in turn, so on weekdays trading barely stops. The busier the hours, the larger the moves; the quiet hours are thin. Big changes can happen while the stock market is closed.

Example

The deepest trading comes in the 4 hours where Europe and America overlap.
