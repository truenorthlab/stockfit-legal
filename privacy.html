Privacy Policy

StockFit ("the App") is provided by TrueNorthLabs Inc. ("we"). This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. What we do not collect

The App has no account sign-up and collects no personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Bookmarks, recently viewed terms, quiz progress, your holding records (purchase prices and share counts), and display settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers. They are removed when you delete the App. We operate no servers and hold no user information. Purchases and payment are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment details. Images you choose in Find in a Screenshot (including images passed from the system's visual search) and questions you type in Ask in Your Words are processed only on your device (including its on-device AI) and are never saved or sent.

2. Tracking

The App does not track you. It includes no analytics or other third-party services, and we do not collect information about how you use it.
